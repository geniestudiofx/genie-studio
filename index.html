The plugins

Two tools, both free

Standalone app

CRT Genie

Drop in your footage and give it the look of an old CRT TV or film: scanlines, glow, colour bleed, curvature and grain. Then export it back out.

  • CRT and film-look presets
  • Live preview while you tweak
  • No editing software needed
Windows 10 / 11.exe
See screenshots
Premiere Pro & After Effects plugin

Element Genie

3D text, logos and models right in your timeline. Click Scene Setup, design your title, press OK, then keyframe it in Effect Controls.

  • 28 materials, 10 bevel styles, 18 icons
  • Soft shadows, reflections, motion blur
  • Import OBJ, FBX, glTF models and SVG logos

Install

Up and running in a minute

Step 1

Download

Grab the installer for the tool you want from the buttons above.

Step 2

Run the installer

Click Yes when Windows asks. If SmartScreen appears, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Step 3

Open and create

CRT Genie opens on its own. For Element Genie, restart Premiere and find it under Effects › Element Genie.
