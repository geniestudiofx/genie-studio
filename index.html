How it works

Four steps from VOD to video

Real screenshots from Clip Genie, made from one of our own streams.

1

Paste a link

Pick an editing style, then paste a Twitch VOD, a Twitch clip or a video file. Only want part of a long VOD? Pick a start and end time.

2

Pick the moments

Clip Genie listens to you, reads your chat and checks viewer clips, then ranks the best moments with a reason for each. Tick the ones you want.

3

Tweak it

Every TikTok opens in the editor: look, subtitles, chat, kill feed, sound, cuts and a timeline. You see the 9:16 video right there.

4

Export

Title and caption are written for you. Save the MP4 to your PC, or scan a QR code to get it straight onto your phone.

What it does for you

Edited like a real editor would

Finds your webcam

Crops your camera and the gameplay into a clean split, or goes full-webcam for talking clips. Decided per clip, automatically.

Cuts the dead air

Pauses and filler words go. Gunshots, kills and the payoff stay, and the ending gets room to land.

Titles on the line

A short title sits right where the webcam meets the gameplay, with your channel name tagged on every video.

CS2 kill feed

Reads the kill feed from your stream and redraws it sharp and clean, with the right guns and headshots.

Subtitles & chat

Optional subtitles in different styles, other voices in their own colour, and the one chat message that explains the clip.

Runs on your PC

Speech-to-text and rendering happen on your own computer, using your graphics card when it can.

Built-in AI

Finds the funny bits, writes titles and edits on request ("make it longer"). Already inside the app: no API key, nothing to pay.

Learns your taste

Thumbs up or down on moments and Clip Genie learns what you like, so the picks get better every stream.

Install it

  1. Download "Clip Genie Setup" from the GitHub page (under Assets).
  2. Double-click it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. The installer isn't signed yet.
  3. Answer 3 quick questions: your Twitch name, music and sounds. No account, no login.
  4. Paste a VOD or clip link and press Make TikToks. The first one takes longer because it downloads the speech model once.

Found a problem?

Open an issue on GitHub with what went wrong, the stream link and roughly when. If something breaks, also attach the file %APPDATA%\GenieStudioFX\ClipGenie\engine.log. Paste that line into the File Explorer address bar to find it.
